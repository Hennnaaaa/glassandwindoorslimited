## Notes on substitutions

- **services/sealed-units/** — Literal "sealed unit" (insulated glass unit) product photography
  is not available as free stock photography, so this category uses close-up stacked glass
  sheets and glass-cutting/workshop imagery as a reasonable stand-in for glazing/glass
  production, per the brief.
- **services/roof-lanterns/** — Genuine UK "roof lantern" product photography (the raised
  glazed box seen on flat-roof kitchen extensions) was not available on Pexels/Pixabay, so this
  category uses residential skylight/roof-window interior shots (bright rooms lit from
  above through glass) as the closest on-topic substitute.
- **services/tilt-turn-windows/cover.jpg** — Shows a window mechanically opened in the
  tilt position (top-hinged, swung inward), which is the clearest available free-stock
  illustration of the tilt & turn opening mechanism.
- **Cover images are landscape-oriented site-wide.** Every service's `cover.jpg` (the
  "main" image used in hero panels, service grids and category cards) was chosen or
  swapped to be landscape, so it reads as a proper wide photo rather than a portrait crop
  jammed into a wide box. `gallery-*.jpg` files were left in their original orientation —
  only cover images were required to be landscape. Where a swap was needed and a
  landscape photo already existed among that service's own assets, the cover and gallery
  files were simply swapped (casement-windows, sliding-doors, sealed-units) rather than
  sourcing a new photo. Where no landscape option existed in the existing set
  (tilt-turn-windows, french-doors), a new landscape photo was sourced from Pexels.
- **hero/hero-2.jpg, hero-3.jpg** and **hero/hero-portrait-2.jpg** are used together on the
  homepage hero (`src/components/sections/Hero.jsx`) as a crossfading background behind
  the 3D glass model — the landscape pair on sm+ screens, the single portrait photo below
  that breakpoint, so mobile never force-crops a landscape hero photo into a tall frame.
  hero-1.jpg, hero-4.jpg and the original hero-portrait-1.jpg were removed per feedback
  and deleted from this folder.
- **sealedUnitTypes** (the 7 "Sealed Units" sub-category pages in `src/data/services.js`)
  mostly reuse cover photos from the services above by path rather than holding separate
  files — acoustic-laminated-glass points at services/tilt-turn-windows/cover.jpg;
  georgian-bar-glass at services/casement-windows/cover.jpg; obscure-glass at
  services/composite-doors/cover.jpg; solar-control-glass at
  services/roof-lanterns/cover.jpg. Swapping one of those services' cover file therefore
  also updates the sealed-unit type that references it. double-glazed-units keeps
  services/sealed-units/cover.jpg (the glass-cutting photo); triple-glazed-units and
  failed-unit-replacement each have their own dedicated file
  (triple-glazed-cover.jpg, failed-unit-cover.jpg) so all three no longer share one image.
- All images were downloaded directly from Pexels' CDN (`images.pexels.com`) at
  1600px width (hero/about) or 1200px width (services), verified as valid JPEG files
  (correct magic bytes, tens-to-hundreds of KB in size) before being committed to this
  folder.
- No image was sourced from, or based on, dgsgroup.co.uk or any other competitor site.
